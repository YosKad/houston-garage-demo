Local know-how

Four seasons, and every one of them tests your garage door

Richmond gets real winters. The first hard freeze of the season is when worn torsion springs snap, and ice storms knock out power and leave openers useless. Summer humidity swells wooden doors in the Fan and Museum District, and late-summer tropical storms push water under worn seals.

The housing stock is varied too: 1920s brick homes with detached alley garages in the city, Colonials and Cape Cods across Henrico and Chesterfield, and newer subdivisions in Short Pump, Midlothian and Mechanicsville with builder-grade springs and openers that are now reaching the end of their life.

Straight answers

A Richmond service you can check up on

RVA Garage Door Rescue is a free matching service. We don't send our own crews. When you call, a coordinator figures out what's wrong, checks which independent garage door companies in our network cover your ZIP code, and connects you with the one who can get there soonest.

In Virginia, contractors must be licensed by the Board for Contractors for most jobs, and their license number belongs on their estimate. Ask your technician for it; the pros we work with expect the question. We follow up after every job, and that feedback decides who keeps getting our calls.

Contractors pay us a referral fee. You never pay us anything, and it doesn't change your quote.

Questions people ask before they call

How fast can someone come out?

In most Greater Richmond ZIP codes we can connect you with a technician the same day. During an ice storm or the first hard freeze, demand spikes, so call early for the best window.

Do your technicians work on old wooden and carriage-house doors?

Yes. Several pros in our network specialize in older homes, including detached alley garages and wooden swing or carriage doors common in the Fan and Museum District.

Are the contractors licensed?

Virginia requires a Board for Contractors license for most garage door jobs. Pros in our network are expected to carry the license their work requires; ask for the number on your estimate.

Is there a fee to use your service?

No. Matching is free for homeowners. You only pay the contractor for work you approve.

Can I open my garage door if a spring breaks in the cold?

Please don't. With a broken spring the door can weigh well over 100 pounds and fall. Leave it closed, keep everyone clear and call for a technician.
