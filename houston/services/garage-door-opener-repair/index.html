Quick answer

Most opener problems in Houston come down to misaligned safety sensors, a worn drive gear or a logic board damaged by heat or a power surge. If the opener is over 12–15 years old, a new belt-drive smart opener is often the better buy.

The usual suspects

When an opener acts up, technicians check the same short list:

  • Safety sensors (photo eyes). Two small units near the floor on either side of the door. In Houston, the low afternoon sun on west-facing garages can blind them and stop the door from closing.
  • Drive gear. Chain and screw-drive openers use a nylon gear that wears down. The motor runs, but nothing moves.
  • Logic board. Heat in a closed-up Texas garage regularly hits 110°F+, and lightning-season power surges can fry boards. Symptoms: dead keypad, erratic lights, no response.
  • Remotes and keypads. Dead batteries, lost pairing, or a code reset needed.
  • Travel and force limits. If the door reverses before it touches the floor, the limits may need adjusting.

What a new installation includes

Removing the old unit, mounting the new opener and rail, new safety sensors and wall control, programming remotes and your phone app, and setting force and travel limits. Expect two to three hours. Ask about battery backup, which lets you open the door during a power outage after a storm.

Before you call: make sure the door itself moves freely. An opener can’t fix a broken spring or a door that’s off track, and running it against a stuck door can burn out the motor.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my garage door open but not close?

Nine times out of ten it's the safety sensors near the floor. If one is bumped out of alignment, dirty, or blinded by direct afternoon sun, the opener won't close the door. Wipe the lenses and check that both LEDs are solid. If that doesn't fix it, a tech can rewire or replace them.

Should I repair or replace my garage door opener?

Repair it if the opener is under 10 years old and the fix is a gear, sensor or remote. Replace it if it's 12+ years old, has a failed logic board, or lacks the rolling-code security and battery backup newer models have.

Can you install a smart garage door opener?

Yes. Technicians in our network install Wi-Fi openers from the major brands, with phone control, alerts if the door is left open and package delivery integration. Many also offer battery backup for power outages, useful during storm season.

My opener hums but the door doesn't move. What's wrong?

Check for a broken spring first. If the springs are fine, a stripped drive gear or a failed capacitor is likely. Both are common repairs.
